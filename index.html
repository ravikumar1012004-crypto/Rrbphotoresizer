RRB NTPC Photo & Signature Upload Guidelines

Agar aap Railway RRB NTPC ka form bhar rahe hain, toh photo aur signature ka size official guidelines ke hisaab se hona bahut zaroori hai. Thodi si bhi galti (jaise blur photo ya galat size) se aapka application form reject ho sakta hai.

1. RRB NTPC Photo Rules

  • Size Limit: Photo ka file size 30KB se 70KB ke beech hona chahiye.
  • Dimensions: 35mm (Width) x 45mm (Height).
  • Background: Photo ka background bilkul White (safed) ya halke rang ka hona chahiye.
  • Rules: Photo me chashma (spectacles/glasses), topi (cap), ya mask nahi pehanna hai. Chehra aur dono kaan (ears) saaf dikhne chahiye.
  • Format: Sirf JPG ya JPEG format allow hai.

2. RRB NTPC Signature Rules

  • Size Limit: Signature ka file size 20KB se 50KB ke beech hona chahiye.
  • Dimensions: 50mm (Width) x 20mm (Height).
  • Ink: Ek safed (white) paper par Black (kaale) ya Blue (neele) pen se running hand me sign karein.
  • Warning: Pura signature Capital letters (BLOCK LETTERS) me nahi hona chahiye, warna form reject ho jayega.
